readouts.js — eye check

No server, no robot. Every component below is driven by synthetic values, including nulls and NaNs, warning and stop conditions, and a stale link. Nothing on a healthy screen should be red, amber or green — press the buttons to make colour appear, and it should be the only thing that changes.

This page loads readouts.js as an ES module, which browsers refuse to do over file://. Serve the directory instead: python3 -m http.server -d Christian_control/tools/panel/static 8001 and open http://localhost:8001/readouts.test.html.

1 · createSlot — the primitive

  • null and NaN show dimmed dashes, never 0.000.
  • Under sweep, the labels to the right of each slot must not move at all, however the digits change.
  • wider than reserved is the counter-example and is meant to push its label sideways: it is what a slot sized for the value you expected, rather than the widest one possible, does on the day it matters.

2 · createErrorRow — bands named by consequence

  • The orientation row shows an arrival mark and the words no stop threshold, and no invented stop mark.
  • Crossing arrival colours nothing: leaving the tolerance is what normal motion looks like.
  • The worst-so-far marker is a ghosted tick and its own dimmed number, and it never replaces the live value.
  • Band marks stay in exactly the same place in every scenario.

3 · createJointBar — true travel, and no invented limits

  • J1, J3, J5, J7 have no range bar, no distance to a limit, and say continuous — no limit.
  • J2, J4, J6 bars are drawn to their real ranges, so the bars are visibly different widths of travel, not a shared 0–100.
  • Under raw 0–360 feedback the bounded joints still land in sensible places: 250.997 is −109.0 on J2's bar.
  • The pale blue tick is the commanded angle. It is the same blue the scene uses for what was asked for.
  • Hovering a bar or tick names the threshold it came from.

4 · createBanner — state as text, marked derived

  • The state word is readable from across the room without colour.
  • derived sits beside the state; reported sits beside the controller, which came from the CSV preamble.
  • STOPPING says there is no ramp. FAULT points at controller.log.
  • With no session, progress and remaining are dashes, not zeros.

5 · createStaleness — the hard break at 2 s

  • The age is always on screen and always counting.
  • At 2.00 s the break is unmistakable, and the region above greys out because isStale() said so.
  • “The panel lost the controller” and “I lost the panel” both reach the break and say different things.
  • A stop button, were one here, would be untouched by any of this.

6 · the spellings panel.js uses

panel.js was written from the same design at the same time and names a few options differently — digits, value in a band, continuous, breakAfterS — and calls set() where this module says update(). Below, every component is built and driven that way instead. It must look the same as the sections above; if it does not, the two files have drifted.

  • Numbers, bands and bars are present, not blank.
  • The joint row built from a flat continuous: true object draws no bar, exactly as section 3 does.
